Delete Your Evolyn Account

You can use this page to learn how to request deletion of your Evolyn account and the data associated with it.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Evolyn and go to Profile & Settings → Account Center → Delete account.
  2. Review the deletion information and confirm your request.
  3. You may optionally tell us why you are deleting your account. Providing a reason is not required.
Important: 24-hour pending period

After you submit an in-app deletion request, it remains pending for 24 hours. If you sign in to your Evolyn account during this period, the deletion request will be cancelled automatically. If you do not sign in, deletion will proceed after the pending period. Once deletion is completed, your account cannot be recovered.

Request deletion if you cannot access Evolyn

Email us from the email address associated with your Evolyn account. Include your account email and state that you want your account and associated data deleted. Do not send your password. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account.

Email an account-deletion request

Data deleted

After deletion is completed, Evolyn will permanently delete the account and associated data stored for your account, including, where applicable:

  • your account profile information, including your first name and email address;
  • your onboarding questionnaire responses;
  • your study-session records, progress history, goals, and preferences;
  • your roadmap data;
  • your notification preferences and reminder schedules; and
  • account-linked identifiers and push-notification registration data associated with your account.

Optional deletion feedback

If you choose to provide a deletion reason in the app, Evolyn stores the reason you submit and the user ID associated with the request as a deletion-feedback record. You can submit a deletion request without providing a reason. This feedback record is retained separately and is not included in the account-associated data deleted when account deletion is completed.
