Privacy Policy

Evolyn · Effective date: October 29, 2026

For privacy questions, data requests, or account-deletion help, contact evolyn33@gmail.com.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Evolyn handles information when you use our study-tracking and productivity app. Evolyn provides features that may include a study timer and timer overlay, study-session and progress tracking, onboarding-based career recommendations, personalized study roadmaps, AI chat, daily study reminders, weekly recaps, and product-update notifications.

2. Information we handle

  • Account information. When you register, we process your first name, email address, an account identifier, and the authentication credential needed to sign in. Your password is used for authentication and is not intentionally included in AI prompts.
  • Onboarding responses. We process the answers you provide to the onboarding questionnaire, such as study preferences, learning interests, and career preferences. These answers help personalize Evolyn. Even when a questionnaire does not ask for a name or email address, answers linked to an account may still be information about that user.
  • Study and roadmap information. Depending on the features you use, we process study-session records, session duration or timing, progress, goals, and the career recommendations or roadmaps generated for you.
  • Notification settings and delivery information. We process which notification categories you enable or disable, and the schedule you choose for daily reminders and weekly recaps. Firebase Cloud Messaging may process a push-notification registration token and related delivery metadata so notifications can reach your device.
  • Technical and service information. Our app infrastructure and service providers may process information needed to authenticate requests, operate the app, store your data, send notifications, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors. Depending on the service and configuration, this may include request timestamps, app or device information, installation identifiers, IP addresses, and diagnostic information.
  • Support and deletion feedback. If you email us, we process the information in your message to respond. If you choose to submit an optional account-deletion reason in the app, we retain the reason and the associated user ID in a separate deletion-feedback record, as explained in Section 8.

3. AI features and information shared with AI providers

Evolyn uses third-party AI services for particular features. AI requests are sent to those providers to generate responses. We do not intentionally include your registration first name, email address, password, or internal Evolyn account ID in AI prompts. This describes the intended data flow; it depends on the app continuing to construct requests in that way.

Gemini API — career recommendations and roadmap generation

When you request a career recommendation or a study roadmap, Evolyn sends relevant onboarding answers and the context needed for that request to Google's Gemini API. We use that information to generate a recommendation or roadmap for you. The request does not need your registration credentials or direct account identifiers. Onboarding answers can nevertheless reveal information about your preferences and interests, even when direct identifiers are omitted.

Google's processing of prompts and responses depends on the Gemini API service tier and configuration being used. Under Google's terms for unpaid Gemini API services, Google may use submitted content and generated responses to improve and develop its products and machine-learning technologies, and human reviewers may read, annotate, or process that content. Under Google's terms for paid Gemini API services, prompts and responses are not used to improve Google's products, but may be logged for a limited period for safety, security, prohibited-use detection, and legal requirements. Google also advises developers not to submit personal, sensitive, or confidential information to unpaid Gemini API services. Review Google's current Gemini API terms for details.

OpenRouter — AI chat

When you use the AI chat feature, Evolyn sends your chat message and the generated roadmap context needed to answer your question through OpenRouter to the selected model provider. This allows the model to answer questions in the context of your roadmap. We do not intentionally add your registration name, email address, password, or internal Evolyn account ID to that request.

OpenRouter routes requests to third-party model providers. Data-handling practices differ by provider and model. A provider may retain prompts or responses, or use them to train, evaluate, or improve its models, depending on its policies and the routing and privacy settings in effect. OpenRouter may also process request metadata such as the model used, timestamps, and token counts. We do not promise that every free model provider has identical retention or training practices. Read OpenRouter's Privacy Policy and the applicable model provider's terms for more information.

Please keep AI chats free of personal or confidential details.

Do not enter your full name, email address, phone number, address, passwords, account credentials, government identifiers, financial details, health information, or confidential information about yourself or another person into the AI chat. Anything you type may be transmitted to OpenRouter and the selected model provider. The app is designed not to send your sign-up credentials automatically, but it cannot prevent you from typing identifying details into a message.

AI-generated recommendations, roadmaps, and chat responses may be incomplete or incorrect. Use your judgment and verify important education or career decisions using reliable sources.

4. Study timer overlay and notifications

Study timer overlay. If you grant Android's “display over other apps” permission, Evolyn can show the study timer as an overlay while you use another app. The overlay permission is used to display the timer. By itself, it does not give Evolyn access to the contents of other apps. This description assumes Evolyn does not separately use Accessibility Services, screen-capture APIs, or other app-monitoring features to inspect activity in other apps.

Notifications. Evolyn can send daily reminders, weekly recaps, and product updates. You can switch these categories on or off in Evolyn's settings and choose the available reminder schedule for daily reminders and weekly recaps. Android may separately ask for notification permission, and you can change that permission through your device settings. If you disable a category or system notifications, delivery may stop for the affected messages.

You can decline or revoke optional permissions. Features that rely on a permission may not work when it is disabled.

  • create, authenticate, secure, and manage your account;
  • provide study timers, study tracking, progress history, and related productivity features;
  • personalize career recommendations and study roadmaps using onboarding answers;
  • provide AI chat responses using the message you submit and relevant roadmap context;
  • save your preferences and schedule the notifications you choose to receive;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve Evolyn and prevent misuse; and
  • respond to support requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

6. Service providers and other disclosures

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications and may use other configured Firebase services to support account, storage, or app functionality. We use Google's Gemini API for career recommendations and roadmap generation, and OpenRouter with third-party model providers for AI chat. These providers process the information required for their respective functions under their own terms, privacy policies, technical configurations, and applicable laws.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to address security or abuse, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer where legally permitted. We do not sell personal information. We do not intentionally include direct account identifiers in AI prompts, as described in Section 3.

7. Storage, security, and international processing

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. Information is transmitted to our service providers as needed to operate Evolyn and its AI features. No system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Our service providers and model providers may process information in countries where they or their infrastructure operate. The applicable provider's terms and privacy practices govern its processing, subject to applicable law.

8. Retention and account deletion

We retain account information, onboarding answers, study records, roadmaps, notification settings, and related records for as long as needed to provide Evolyn, maintain account functionality, protect the service, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

You can request account deletion inside Evolyn by opening Profile & Settings → Account Center → Delete account. An in-app deletion request has a 24-hour pending period. If you sign in during that period, the request is cancelled automatically. If you do not sign in, deletion proceeds after the pending period, and the account cannot be recovered once deletion is completed. You can also read the account-deletion instructions or email evolyn33@gmail.com if you cannot access the app. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account; never send us your password.

When deletion is completed, we delete the account and associated data held in our active systems, including account profile information, onboarding answers, study-session records, roadmap data, notification preferences, reminder schedules, and account-linked push-notification data, where applicable. Certain limited records may be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, or legal claims.

Optional deletion-feedback record. If you choose to provide a deletion reason, the reason and associated user ID are kept in a separate deletion-feedback record and are not removed as part of the account-associated data deletion described above. The reason is optional; you can request account deletion without providing it. We retain this separate record only for as long as reasonably necessary for deletion-feedback and service-monitoring purposes, then delete or de-identify it. Please do not include sensitive or identifying details in the optional reason.

Third-party AI processing. Account deletion from Evolyn does not necessarily delete prompts or responses already processed or retained by Google, OpenRouter, or a model provider under that provider's own policies. We do not intentionally send your registration credentials or internal Evolyn account ID with AI prompts, but the provider's retention rules may apply to the request content that was sent. Provider-managed records are subject to the applicable provider's terms and retention practices.

Removing Evolyn from your device does not itself delete your account or server-stored data.
